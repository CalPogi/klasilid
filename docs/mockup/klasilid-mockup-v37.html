What if the Wi-Fi goes down?

The reader keeps every tap with its time and sends them once the Wi-Fi is back. Nothing is lost.

What if there's a brownout?

Taps already saved stay saved. The reader starts again by itself when the power returns.

A student lost their card?

Register a new card from Students. The old card stops working right away.

Want one for your class?

Other advisers can get the same door reader, set up and ready to use with Klasilid.

  • RFID door reader with a small screen and beep, in a wall-mount case
  • Power adapter. It runs all day, every day, no laptop needed
  • 40 student ID cards (more available)
  • Setup at your school: Wi-Fi, mounting, and registering every card
  • Works without internet: built-in clock, backup battery, and its own Wi-Fi so your phone still sees taps
  • Connects to your Klasilid account for seat plan, attendance and SF2
