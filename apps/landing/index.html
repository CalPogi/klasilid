Attendance that takes itself.

Students tap their card at the classroom door. Klasilid marks them on time or late, keeps the seat plan, and works even when the school internet is down.

Download for Android Download for Windows Open in browser

Android 7 or newer · Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · iPhone: use Open in browser.

Tap in, tap out

A small reader by the door records each card tap with the right time, morning and afternoon.

No internet? Still works

The reader saves every tap and keeps time on its own, then sends them once the internet is back.

Made for the adviser

Today's attendance, the month's record, the seat plan and the school calendar in one place.

Installing on Windows

  1. Open Klasilid-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Klasilid isn't in the Microsoft Store yet.

Installing on Android

  1. Open Klasilid.apk from your downloads.
  2. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install. Klasilid isn't on Google Play yet.
