Privacy notice

This notice explains what Klasilid keeps about learners, parents and teachers, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be corrected or removed.

Who is responsible

[RESPONSIBLE] is responsible for the information in Klasilid.

What Klasilid keeps

  • Learners: name, sex, LRN, class and seat, and the card number used at the door.
  • Attendance: the time of every card tap, the day's status (on time, late, absent, excused), and any correction with its reason.
  • Excuse letters: a photo of the letter, when the teacher adds one.
  • Parents or guardians: name and phone number, when the teacher adds them.
  • Accounts: the username, display name and, if given, an email for password resets.

Who can see it

The learner's teacher. Later, a learner's own parent or guardian and the learner themselves will be able to see that learner's attendance, and only theirs.

Where it's kept

On Supabase servers in Singapore, and on the teacher's own devices so attendance keeps working without internet. Klasilid doesn't sell or share this information and shows no ads.

How long

For the current school year plus one year, then deleted.

Your choices

To ask for a copy of a learner's information, a correction, or removal, contact [CONTACT].
