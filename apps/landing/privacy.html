Privacy notice

This notice explains what Klasilid keeps about learners, parents and teachers, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be corrected or removed.

Who is responsible

[RESPONSIBLE] is responsible for the information in Klasilid.

What Klasilid keeps

  • Learners: full name (with middle name), sex, LRN, class and seat, the card number used at the door, and when they joined or left the class, with the school they came from or moved to.
  • Attendance: the time of every card tap (including taps of cards not yet given to a learner), the day's status (on time, late, absent, excused), and any correction with its reason.
  • Excuse letters: a photo of the letter, when the teacher adds one.
  • Parents or guardians: name and phone number, when the teacher adds them.
  • Accounts: the username and display name, and an email address only if the account holder adds one for password resets.
  • A history of changes: who changed a record, when, and what it was before, so mistakes can be traced and undone.

Who can see it

The learner's teacher. Later, a learner's own parent or guardian and the learner themselves will be able to see that learner's attendance, and only theirs.

Where it's kept

On Supabase servers in Singapore, and on the teacher's own devices so attendance keeps working without internet. Klasilid doesn't sell or share this information and shows no ads.

This website and the app load their typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens. No learner information is sent to Google.

How long

For the current school year plus one year, then deleted.

Your choices

To ask for a copy of a learner's information, a correction, or removal, contact [CONTACT].
